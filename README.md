# WiseSell AI

## AI-Powered Business Platform for Ethiopia and East Africa

WiseSell AI helps businesses manage operations, sell products, and grow through artificial intelligence.

### Core Features
- AI Business Assistant
- AI Sales and Marketing
- Product and Inventory Management
- Online Business Marketplace
- Sales and Expense Analytics
- Subscription Management
- Multilingual Support: English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, and Somali

### Social Media Marketing
Prepare platform-specific marketing content for:
- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- TikTok
- Facebook
- Instagram
- X (Twitter)
- LinkedIn

### Technology
- Frontend: Web application
- Backend: Supabase
- AI: OpenAI API
